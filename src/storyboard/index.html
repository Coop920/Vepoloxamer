The Thesis

LifeRaft Biosciences is advancing Vepoloxamer, a clinically tested therapeutic with a strong safety profile that targets cell membrane damage — a key driver of death and disability across multiple high-value indications. Starting with Radiation-Induced Severe Oral Mucositis for patients undergoing radiation therapy for head and neck cancer, a >$1B market with significant unmet need, the company has a clear and capital-efficient path to proof of concept. Novel Use of Vepoloxamer

Radiation-induced severe oral mucositis for patients undergoing radiation therapy for head and neck cancer. Approximately 90,000 patients a year across the US, EU and UK face radiation therapy with no FDA-approved drug to prevent its most debilitating side effect. 60–70% develop severe, Grade 3+ disease.

De-Risked Molecule

Vepoloxamer, a purified form of P188, physically reseals damaged cell membranes. The drug was used in prior clinical trials for sickle cell and cardiac programs, with more than 2,300 people having been treated with the drug. Adverse events were comparable to placebo and no drug-related deaths. This prior clinical exposure reduces the safety, manufacturing, and regulatory risk that typical first-in-human drugs face, and which Vepoloxamer does not.

Our Approach →

Not a discovery-stage compound.

Vepoloxamer is a non-ionic triblock copolymer that has been manufactured and administered to patients in multiple Phase 3 trials. What changes with LifeRaft is the development approach: we are the first company to advance it as a radioprotectant, administering the drug prior to scheduled radiation in order to protect healthy tissue. This is an indication where the biology, the patient population, and the clinical endpoint all align.
